## CJK punctuation boundaries

```````````````````````````````` example
例子例子**"加粗"**例子例子
.
<p>例子例子<strong>&quot;加粗&quot;</strong>例子例子</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
日本語**(強調)**日本語
.
<p>日本語<strong>(強調)</strong>日本語</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
한국어**[강조]**한국어
.
<p>한국어<strong>[강조]</strong>한국어</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
**来源：**2013年
.
<p><strong>来源：</strong>2013年</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Go**「初心者」**を対象とした記事です。
.
<p>Go<strong>「初心者」</strong>を対象とした記事です。</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
.NET**（.NET Frameworkは不可）**では、
.
<p>.NET<strong>（.NET Frameworkは不可）</strong>では、</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
〽︎**(庵点)**は、
.
<p>〽︎<strong>(庵点)</strong>は、</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
禰󠄀**(ね)**豆子
.
<p>禰󠄀<strong>(ね)</strong>豆子</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Underscore forms

```````````````````````````````` example
__注意__：注意事項
.
<p><strong>注意</strong>：注意事項</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
注意：__注意事項__
.
<p>注意：<strong>注意事項</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Strikethrough

```````````````````````````````` example
a~~a()~~あ
.
<p>a<del>a()</del>あ</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
「~~a」~~a
.
<p>「<del>a」</del>a</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
~~日本語。~~English.
.
<p><del>日本語。</del>English.</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Non-regressions

```````````````````````````````` example
a * foo bar*
.
<p>a * foo bar*</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a_foo bar_
.
<p>a_foo bar_</p>
````````````````````````````````
